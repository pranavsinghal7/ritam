    Ask about your heart records

    Ask in your own words. The assistant can see your age, health conditions, readings and heart score. It cannot see your name, email, contact or medicine names. It explains; it does not diagnose or decide your alert level.

    Get medical help now

    Your readings look unusual for you.

      Call 112 right away if you have chest pain or pressure, pain in the arm, jaw or back, trouble breathing, a cold sweat, or you feel faint. Sit down and stay still until help arrives.

      If you feel completely fine, sit down, rest for a few minutes and measure again. Movement, cold fingers and poor lighting can give false readings.
